OUR 15-YEAR HERITAGE

Born in Tiruppur, Loved Worldwide

Founded in 2011 on Dharapuram Road, Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu — India's famous textile city — Astah Boutique was started with one simple mission: to give every woman custom-made clothes that fit her body like a dream.

15 Years of Master Tailoring

With 15 continuous years of tailoring experience in Tiruppur, our master cutting masters know every body type and ensure zero loose shoulders, no wrinkles, and a comfortable, flattering fit.

Authentic Hand Aari & Maggam Work

We do not use cheap machine prints. Our in-house artisans sit at traditional wooden Maggam frames to handcraft gold zari, pearls, cut-beads, and stones into your bridal blouses.

Online Orders & Global Delivery

Can't visit our Tiruppur shop in person? We take your measurements over WhatsApp video calls and courier safely across India, USA, UK, Singapore, Malaysia, and the Middle East.

IN-HOUSE WORKSHOP

Where 15 Years of Craft Lives

Every garment is cut and stitched directly in our Dharapuram Road workshop under the personal supervision of our senior master tailor.
